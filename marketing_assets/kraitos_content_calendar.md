# Kraitos Content Calendar

Current date: 2026-06-10

This calendar turns the Kraitos rename into a repeatable CMO rhythm: video reach, launch, proof, trust, and contributor activation. Use Postiz for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X, and LinkedIn scheduling; keep Reddit posts manual until a subreddit, title, and integration are confirmed.

## Channel Roles

- **Instagram Reels**: Primary visual discovery channel for the desktop-agent/Jarvis-style concept.
- **TikTok**: Highest-velocity testing channel for hooks, demo framing, and short proof clips.
- **YouTube Shorts**: Evergreen video discovery channel; every strong short should land here.
- **Facebook Reels/Page**: Secondary reach and retargeting surface, especially for broader automation interest.
- **X**: Short technical hooks, demos, architecture proof, and project-page traffic.
- **LinkedIn**: Systems-level framing for founders, engineers, and operators.
- **Reddit**: Long-form self-hosting feedback and architecture critique.
- **HyperFrames**: Proof assets for browser bridge, OCR fallback, and remote control flows.

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

Monday:
- Ship one vertical demo clip first; adapt it to X/LinkedIn only after the short-form hook is clear.
- Review Postiz analytics from the previous week.
- Update `schedule_log.json` with IDs and results.

Wednesday:
- Publish one architecture explanation and one cut-down vertical proof clip.
- Ask one precise feedback question.
- Reply to every technical comment with implementation details from `kraitos_engagement_playbook.md`.

Friday:
- Publish one trust-building vertical short: failure mode, limitation, recovery path, or security boundary.
- Convert strong replies into docs tasks, demo tasks, or future public issues after the repo URL is verified.
