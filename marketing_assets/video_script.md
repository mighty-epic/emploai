# EmploAI Visual Demo Script & Storyboard

This document contains the script and timing storyboard for the EmploAI promotional video. This script is designed to be rendered into an MP4 video using the **Hyperframes** framework.

---

## Storyboard & Timing

*   **Total Duration**: 20 seconds
*   **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Landscape)
*   **Background**: Smooth dark gradient with a subtle grain overlay
*   **Font Family**: `Outfit` and `Inter` (Google Fonts)

| Time (Seconds) | Visual Scene | On-Screen Text Content | Transition / Motion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0.0s – 3.5s** | **Scene 1: Introduction** | "Meet EmploAI" <br> *Sub:* "The Agentic OS Control Layer" | Fade in and scale up from center; glowing indigo backdrop. |
| **3.5s – 7.5s** | **Scene 2: Core Concept** | "Control your machine from anywhere." <br> *Sub:* "Accepts voice & text via Electron & Telegram" | Slide in from right; display phone & desktop mockups/logos. |
| **7.5s – 11.5s** | **Scene 3: The Extension Bridge** | "Chrome Extension WebSocket Bridge" <br> *Sub:* "Bypasses bot-detection using active user sessions" | Sweep highlight under title; text fades in letter-by-letter. |
| **11.5s – 15.5s** | **Scene 4: Desktop Fallbacks & OCR** | "OS & Desktop Level Control" <br> *Sub:* "Fails over to coordinates, screenshots, and OCR" | Scale down screen mockup; flash red/green boundary indicators. |
| **15.5s – 20.0s** | **Scene 5: Call to Action** | "Auditable. Secure. Open Source." <br> *Sub:* "github.com/heygen-com/emploai" | Smooth fade out of title; CTA button pulses in center. |
